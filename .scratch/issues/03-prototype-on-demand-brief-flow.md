## Question

What compact visual and interaction design best supports the on-demand flow: prominent URL submission, in-place completed Brief, and secondary saved Brief library, while presenting the public demo library clearly to recruiters?

## Answer

The workbench variant won. Keep a persistent, compact URL submission area and a small saved-brief library alongside the current Brief; make the open Brief the dominant reading surface. This supports ad-hoc creation without turning the product into a library-first dashboard. Prototype branch: `codex/prototype-briefly`. The full variants are preserved there, outside the implementation branch.
